    How a forecast becomes a dispatch
    
      
      
        
    1. Learn. Gradient-boosted models read rainfall, temperature and humidity going back 8 weeks, plus each ward's density, water-logging and sanitation.
      2. 
        
    2. Forecast. A mean and a 10th and 90th percentile for weeks 2, 3 and 4.
      3. 
        
    3. Rank. Priority grows with predicted cases, density, water-logging and poor sanitation.
      4. 
        
    4. Allocate. Saline, test kits and crews go to the highest-ranked wards first.
      5. 
        
    5. Route beds. Overflow patients move to the ward with the most spare beds, inside the same city only.
      6. 
        
    6. Dispatch. SMS, WhatsApp and a printable commissioner brief for every ward.
      7. 
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Unplug the network. It still works.
    
        
    The engine blocks every outbound connection, runs the whole pipeline twice, and checks that nothing leaked and the results are identical.
